Trend Study

A study designed so it could fail, that then failed

Closed - negative result

A pre-registered, fail-closed research study testing twelve trend-following signal variants across two arms with a develop window (2003-2013), a validate window (2014-2018), and a sealed holdout. All variants failed their pre-committed gates, exhausting the variant budget. The sealed holdout was never opened and remains available for future study.

The problem

Trend-following strategies often appear promising in backtests but fail out of sample due to overfitting and multiple-comparison bias. The study needed to test whether a specific construction of trend signals could survive a pre-committed validation bar without parameter tuning after seeing results. The core difficulty was designing a process that could genuinely fail and record that failure rather than iterating until something passed.

What I built

The study closed with a negative result on 2026-09-04. All twelve Arm A variants cleared every gate on the develop window (Sharpe 0.735-0.852 vs 0.61 benchmark) but failed on the validate window (Sharpe -0.037 to +0.341 vs 0.80 benchmark, G1 12/12, G3 12/12, G4 10/12). Arm B never cleared develop. The failure was identical across the entire parameter grid, indicating the approach itself was wrong. The sealed holdout was never opened (0 of 1 grading attempts used) and remains spendable.

How it works

The study pre-registered twelve variant configurations across two arms (A and B) with a fixed variant budget of twelve per arm. Arm A used a specific signal construction tested on a develop window (2003-2013) and validate window (2014-2018); Arm B used an alternative construction. Each variant was graded against three gates (G1, G3, G4) on the validate window. A sealed holdout window was reserved but never opened. The pipeline enforced fail-closed mechanics: once the variant budget was exhausted, no further variants could run under the same registration. SHA-256 hashes locked all inputs and variant rows.

All 12 declared variants failed the validate window; recorded as a negative, holdout unspent

Highlights

  • The study pre-registered twelve variants per arm with a hard budget that could not be exceeded after seeing results.
  • Arm A variants achieved Sharpe ratios of 0.735 to 0.852 on the develop window against a 0.61 benchmark.
  • All twelve Arm A variants failed on the validate window with Sharpe ratios of -0.037 to +0.341 against a 0.80 benchmark.
  • Gate failures on validate were G1 12/12, G3 12/12, and G4 10/12 across the full variant grid.
  • Arm B never cleared the develop window in any of its twelve variants.
  • The sealed holdout window was never opened and remains available for a future study with a new registration.
