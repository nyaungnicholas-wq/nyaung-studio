The problem

Quantitative finance research often overfits to in-sample parameter sweeps, ignores transaction costs, and reports inflated Sharpe ratios without correcting for multiple testing or serial correlation in returns. Standard bootstraps destroy return autocorrelation, yielding falsely narrow confidence intervals. Covariance estimation noise turns risk parity into equal weight silently. Optimizers frequently fail to beat equal-weight portfolios out of sample. These issues make published backtests unreliable and strategies fragile in production.

What I built

All 204 tests pass (core 51, EMA 34, Monte Carlo 44, risk parity 39, Sharpe 36). Monte Carlo converges to Black-Scholes 10.450584 with control variate reducing standard error over 5x. ERC solver equalizes risk contributions to 1e-6. Tangency portfolio beats 20,000 random portfolios on Sharpe; minimum-variance has lowest volatility. Backtests verified free of lookahead via future-price perturbation. Block bootstrap widens correctly with block length on AR(1) series. The optimizer often does not beat equal weight out of sample, which is reported honestly.

How it works

A shared core (qlab/) provides data loading with synthetic GBM fallback, metrics including CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, and guardrails: block bootstrap confidence intervals that preserve serial correlation, deflated Sharpe ratios tracking cumulative trials across all tickers in outputs/trial_ledger.json, and probabilistic Sharpe ratios. EMA crossover uses walk-forward grid search with one-bar signal shift and basis-point turnover costs. Monte Carlo prices European options with antithetic variates and a control variate (discounted stock price) cutting standard error over 5x versus Black-Scholes 10.450584, plus common-random-number Greeks. Risk parity solves the true ERC problem via log-barrier convex optimization with EWMA and Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage covariance, rebalancing monthly with costs. Sharpe optimizer constructs the efficient frontier, tangency and minimum-variance portfolios, a Michaud resampled frontier, and a train/test split versus equal weight.

Highlights

  • A shared core handles data loading, metrics, and plotting with guardrails including block bootstrap confidence intervals and deflated Sharpe ratios that accumulate trial counts across all tickers in a persistent ledger.
  • EMA crossover uses walk-forward evaluation with one-bar signal shift and basis-point turnover costs, reporting the out-of-sample result rather than the best in-sample parameter cell.
  • Monte Carlo option pricing converges to the exact Black-Scholes value 10.450584 using antithetic variates and a control variate that cuts standard error by more than 5x.
  • Risk parity solves the true Equal Risk Contribution problem via log-barrier convex optimization with selectable covariance estimators, equalizing risk contributions to within 1e-6.
  • Sharpe optimizer constructs the efficient frontier with a Michaud resampled frontier and train/test split, and the tangency portfolio must beat 20,000 random portfolios on Sharpe in tests.
  • All 204 tests pass offline on synthetic data, including lookahead verification via future-price perturbation and block bootstrap validation on AR(1) series.
