The problem

Retail investors who want to follow \"smart money\" end up stitching together scattered 13F filings, congressional disclosures, insider forms, and sovereign-fund reports by hand. Any single source can run late, get noisy, or be flat-out wrong. Nothing consolidates the view, shows you who's moving, and forces you to verify a signal before you trade on it.

What I built

Shipped and Built. The dashboard tracks 28+ investors across 7 categories from 9 live data sources. Real-time catalysts run alongside multi-source sentiment and the multi-gate safety checks, all in a single OLED dark interface.

How it works

The dashboard pulls activity from 9 market data sources into one feed. It covers 28+ tracked investors sorted into 7 categories: hedge funds, politicians, insiders, sovereign wealth funds, and more. Two checks sit on top of that feed. Multi-source sentiment keeps any single feed from driving the read, and a multi-gate safety validation framework cross-verifies a signal across gates before it ever surfaces as actionable. The front end is static, built in vanilla JS, HTML5, and CSS3 with no framework. The OLED dark-mode interface uses Fira Sans for the UI and Fira Code for tabular figures, held at 4.5:1 contrast so dense rows of numbers stay legible at a glance.
